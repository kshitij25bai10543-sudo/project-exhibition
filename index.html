METHODOLOGY

HOW WE BUILD IT.

01

PREPROCESSING

Normalize satellite feeds, align telemetry, augment training sets.

02

MODELING

Train ConvLSTM + Spatial Transformer ensembles for spatiotemporal prediction.

03

EVALUATION

Benchmark against historical storm tracks; tune for lead-time reliability.

04

DEPLOYMENT

Serve predictions via FastAPI, render GIS heatmaps and trigger geo-alerts.
